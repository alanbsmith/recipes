# Waffle Cones 🍦

## Yield

~6 cones

## Ingredients

- 2 large egg whites
- ¼ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup sugar
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp almond extract
- 4 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- ⅔ cup all-purpose flour
