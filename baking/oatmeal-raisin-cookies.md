# Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

## Notes

Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 10-12 minutes
Yield: ~18 cookies (using a medium / Size 40 cookie scoop)

> Note: For best results, chill the cookie dough for at least an hour before baking. Baking cookie dough at room temperature will cause the cookies to spread too thin, and make it easier for them to burn.

## Ingredients

### Wet Ingredients

* ½ cup butter (softened)
* ½ cup brown sugar
* ¼ cup white sugar
* 1 egg
* 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Dry Ingredients

* ¾ cup flour
* 1 ½ cup rolled oats
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp cinnamon
* ¼ tsp nutmeg
* ¼ tsp ginger￼
* ¾ cup raisins￼￼ (soaked in warm water)

## Directions

> Note: Preheat oven ~30min before baking

1. Soak raisins in warm water
2. Mix wet ingredients in a large bowl
3. Add and mix dry ingredients
4. Drain raisins and add to mixture
5. Chill the dough for at least one hour
6. Preheat oven to 350°F
7. Scoop into balls and place on a baking sheet (non-stick or with parchment paper)
8. Place in the oven for 10-12 minutes
9. Let cool for 2 minutes and move to cooling rack
10. Enjoy!
